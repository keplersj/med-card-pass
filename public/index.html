Med Card PassRuns on your device

Your card, in your wallet.

Bring your medical card PDF. This page reads its QR code and details on your phone and makes a wallet pass from them.

PreviewCard image, 1080 × 1500

Nothing leaves your phone.

The PDF is opened and the pass is built in this browser tab. No upload, no account.

  1. Choose the PDF

    The card itself, not a receipt or a letter. Drop it on the card space, or pick it here.

  2. Look it over

    Fix anything that was read wrong. The QR code is copied exactly.

    What the QR code says

    nothing yet

  3. Add it to your wallet

    On Android, open the downloaded pass and choose Google Wallet.

    Signed with a test certificate, which Google Wallet accepts. If yours won't, try an unsigned pass, or add the card image with Add to Wallet, Photo.

    On iPhone, open the pass and tap Add.

    iPhone needs an Apple-signed pass, which this copy of the site can't make yet.
