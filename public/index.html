How to add it
  1. Tap Add to wallet to download the pass.
  2. Open it from your notifications or Files.
  3. Choose Google Wallet and tap Continue.

Signed with a test certificate, which Google Wallet accepts. Trouble? Get an unsigned pass, or save the card image and use Add to Wallet, Photo.

iPhone: open the pass and tap Add.

iPhone needs an Apple-signed pass, which this copy of the site can't make yet.

Privacy

Your PDF is read and the pass is built in this browser tab. Nothing about your card leaves your phone.

Open source, MIT. Not affiliated with any state program. Keep your official card too.
