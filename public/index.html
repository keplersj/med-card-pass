Add it to your wallet

Android, Google Wallet:

  1. Tap Download .pkpass.
  2. Open the download from the notification or the Files app.
  3. Choose Google Wallet and tap Add.

If Wallet won't take the pass, try the unsigned one. If neither works, save the card image, then in Google Wallet tap Add to Wallet, Photo (or Everything else) and pick it.

iPhone: tap Download .pkpass, then Add on the pass preview.

iPhone: Apple Wallet only opens passes signed with an Apple pass certificate, and this copy of the site doesn't have one set up yet, so iPhones can't use the test pass.

Your card stays on your phone

Your PDF is read in this browser tab and never uploaded. The pass is built here too. When this site has an Apple certificate, it signs only a list of file fingerprints, never your details; otherwise the test signature is made in your browser. Once you add the pass to a wallet, it lives there like any other pass.
