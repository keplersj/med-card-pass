Add your medical card to your phone's wallet

Choose the PDF of your card. This page reads it on your phone and makes a wallet pass with the same QR code.

1

Choose your card PDF

Or drag the file here. Use the card PDF itself, not a receipt or letter.

2

Check the details

A preview appears here once you choose your card.

Fix anything the page read wrong. The QR code is copied exactly and can't be edited.

3

Add it to your wallet

On Android:

  1. Open the downloaded pass from your notifications or Files.
  2. Choose Google Wallet, then Continue.

This copy of the site signs passes with a test certificate. Google Wallet accepts it. If yours doesn't, download an unsigned pass, or save the card image and add it with Add to Wallet, Photo.

On iPhone: open the pass and tap Add.

On iPhone: Apple Wallet needs an Apple-signed pass, which this copy of the site can't make yet.

Your card stays on your phone. The PDF is read and the pass is built in this browser. Nothing about your card is sent anywhere.
